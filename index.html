About

I am a Senior Lecturer in the School of Economics at the University of Cape Town. My research lies at the intersection of housing, household finance, urban economics, and public finance, with a particular focus on South Africa and other emerging-market economies. I am especially interested in how households, financial institutions, and public policy interact in housing and credit markets, and in using large administrative, transaction, and other micro-level datasets to study these questions. My work also has a strong interest in measurement and the development of new data and empirical approaches for answering policy-relevant economic questions. My research has been published in the Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization and Real Estate Economics. I hold a PhD in Economics from UCT and have spent time as a visiting academic at New York University, the University of Oxford, and Imperial College London.

Alongside my academic research, I work extensively at the interface between research and public policy. I am the Resident Lead Academic for the South Africa programme of the International Growth Centre (IGC), where I help shape and develop a programme of policy-relevant economic research in South Africa. I am also a Research Fellow at UNU-WIDER, where I lead the data workstream of the SA-TIED programme, which uses South African administrative data to answer questions about tax, employment and industrial development. I have previously held the South African Reserve Bank Research Chair in Financial Stability, and have contributed to research and policy work with the National Treasury of South Africa, Statistics South Africa, the South African Reserve Bank, the City of Cape Town, and other public institutions. I am also a Research Fellow in the Department of Economics at Stellenbosch University, where I helped to establish the Centre for Household Finance.

Outside of my research, I have been Director of the Financial Innovation Hub at the University of Cape Town since 2025. The Hub supports research, teaching and industry engagement on financial technology and its implications for financial inclusion and regulation in South Africa.
